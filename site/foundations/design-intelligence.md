<p class="page-intro">
  Design Intelligence sits above principles and heuristics. It evaluates whether a design is appropriate, understandable, and intentional — not just correct.
</p>

<h2 id="process-heading">Evaluation Process</h2>

<p>Always evaluate in this order:</p>

<div class="docs-table-wrap">
  <table class="docs-table">
    <thead>
      <tr>
        <th>Step</th>
        <th>Question</th>
      </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr><td>1. Goal</td><td>Why does this exist? What outcome should it create?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>2. User</td><td>Who is using this? What do they already know?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>3. Context</td><td>Where is this used? Under what conditions?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>4. Constraints</td><td>What cannot change? What must be preserved?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>5. Principles</td><td>Hierarchy, consistency, simplicity, affordance, feedback, balance?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>6. Heuristics</td><td>Visibility, user control, error prevention, recognition, accessibility?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>7. Critique</td><td>Focus, density, rhythm, clarity, trust, cohesion, noise?</td></tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</div>

<h2 id="reasoning-heading">Design Reasoning</h2>

<div class="docs-table-wrap">
  <table class="docs-table">
    <thead>
      <tr>
        <th>Dimension</th>
        <th>Question</th>
      </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr><td>Appropriateness</td><td>Is this suitable for the user, context, and business goal?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Complexity</td><td>Is complexity justified? Can it be reduced?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Trade-Offs</td><td>What is gained? What is lost? What alternatives exist?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Emotional Fit</td><td>Does the response match the desired experience?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Confidence</td><td>Does the interface inspire trust?</td></tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</div>

<h2 id="critique-heading">Design Critique Dimensions</h2>

<div class="docs-table-wrap">
  <table class="docs-table">
    <thead>
      <tr>
        <th>Dimension</th>
        <th>What to evaluate</th>
      </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr><td>Focus</td><td>Is the primary task immediately clear?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Hierarchy</td><td>Can users scan and find what matters?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Density</td><td>Is the information density appropriate for the context?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Rhythm</td><td>Is there consistent spacing and visual cadence?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Clarity</td><td>Are labels, actions, and states unambiguous?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Trust</td><td>Does the design feel reliable and professional?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Cohesion</td><td>Do all elements feel like they belong together?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Visual Noise</td><td>Is there unnecessary decoration or distraction?</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Attention Competition</td><td>Are elements fighting for focus?</td></tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</div>

<h2 id="system-lens-heading">Design System Lens</h2>

<p>Before making any design decision, ask:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Would this decision still make sense if repeated 100 times?</li>
  <li>Can this be reused? Can this scale?</li>
  <li>Can this be documented? Can this be automated?</li>
  <li>Can this become a pattern, token, or guidance?</li>
</ul>

<p>If not — investigate a more systematic solution.</p>
